Glow Up Manifesting - Privacy Policy


This policy applies to all information collected or submitted via the Glow Up Manifesting app on iOS.


Information We Collect


Glow Up Manifesting does not collect any information that can leave your device for its core features. Your Era journal entries, Lucky Girl logs, Vision Studio images, and Mirror Mode recordings remain stored locally on your device until you reset this data or delete the app — none of it is transmitted to us or stored on any server we operate.


Subscriptions


Premium access is offered through auto-renewing subscriptions processed by Apple's App Store. We use Adapty, a third-party subscription management provider, to verify and manage your subscription status. When you purchase or restore a subscription, Adapty receives transaction-related information (such as anonymized device and purchase identifiers) from Apple for this purpose. Adapty never receives your journal entries, logs, images, or recordings. You can review Adapty's own privacy practices at https://adapty.io/privacy/.


Camera and Microphone


Mirror Mode uses your device's camera and microphone, with your permission, to let you record yourself speaking your affirmations. These recordings are saved only to your device and are never transmitted to us.


Notifications


The app may request your permission to send device notifications for daily check-ins and Era reminders. These are scheduled locally on your device.


Children's Privacy


This app is not directed at children under the minimum age required in our Terms and Conditions, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


Your Consent


By using this app, you consent to this privacy policy.


Contacting Us


If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit our support page.


Changes to This Privacy Policy
